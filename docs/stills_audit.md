# Campaign stills audit (2026-11-22)

The owner asked (2026-11-22 10:07) for every campaign still to be checked for hallucinations and imperfections, like
the sprites. All 118 pictures in `campaign/stills/` (59 stills, each with its `_small` copy) were looked at, first on
2x2 contact sheets, then every one at full size.

## Fixed (0.38.128)

- **a02_01 The Guild in Exile**: the city on the horizon through the barn door was the Moscow Kremlin (onion domes, a
  long red crenellated wall), not Nuremberg. Repainted in ChatGPT as an edit of the same painting: the Kaiserburg on its
  hill, the twin spires of St. Lorenz, the sandstone wall and gate towers. Nothing else changed.
- **a04_05 The Broken Ones**: Hollweg stood sunk into the cobbles to the waist, with no legs. Repainted as an edit: he
  stands on the cobbles in breeches, stockings and buckled shoes, the rest as it was.

The originals before the edits are in git history (`art/campaign/stills/a02_01.png`, `a04_05.png`).

## Looked at and kept

The other 57 stills have no extra or missing limbs, doubled weapons, melted faces or garbled text at the size the game
shows them. Small things noticed and left as they are:

- The Crown's soldiers are drawn sometimes with steel morion helmets, sometimes with tricornes: the game's Guardsman
  and Musketeer, so both are right.
- a07_04 The Accused: a cardinal in red sits on the raised chair at the back. He is not the Pope (the Pope is never
  shown); fine as drawn.
- Small brass gear crates at the quay in a05_04 are the mission's one crate hint, as planned.
- Hollweg's face and goggles drift a little between stills (the paintings were made in different ChatGPT accounts);
  readable as the same man.
