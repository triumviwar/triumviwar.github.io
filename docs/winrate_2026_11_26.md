# Win rates 2026-11-26: all six matchups, with and without the Rootmaw nerf

The owner (2026-11-26): "After implementation of the new combat and battle rules and what was learned by the battle tests
into the main AI, I want you to run win rate tests, and then ... win rate tests with the Rootmaw nerf, not only against
Crown but all matchups, so we have a good way to compare."

Main 0.38.221 (ai-lessons, crown-micro, crown-micro-2, crown-finish, brood-micro merged). Hard OPTIMAL v Hard OPTIMAL,
every game to 20:00, the six parity maps, 30 games a cell, the same 30 games (seeds 1126001-1126030) in every cell and
arm. NERF = the match switch `farmaw:2.0` (a far Rootmaw pays x2.0, not x2.2 = Defs.FOOD_BONUS 1.2 -> 1.0); it touches
only the Brood, so the three matchups without the Brood have one arm. Score = income earned + 2 x army at the bell or the
defeat (a beaten side a quarter); led = 10% ahead at the bell. 270 games.

Four part reports with every number: docs/winrate_2026_11_26_crown_brood.md, _clock_brood.md, _brood_mirror.md,
_no_brood.md. Data docs/winrate_2026_11_26/data/. Tools tools/study/winrate_1126_*.py.

## What it shows

1. **The Crown has caught up with the Brood.** On these seeds it used to win or lead about 3 games in 30 (crown-brood-fix
   baseline); now 13 to 16. The Brood still wins outright more (12 v 5).
2. **The nerf makes Crown v Brood even on average but does not change who wins or leads.** The Brood earns ~7% less by
   16:00, wins outright 7 times instead of 12, but leads at the bell more (9 v 4): won or led 12 / 16 either way.
3. **The nerf does not help the Clockwork against the Brood.** About even either way; the start spot matters most there
   (the Clockwork on spot A won or led 12 of 18, on spot B 4 of 12). Spots weighted evenly: 50% default, 42% nerf.
4. **The Crown now beats the Clockwork and finishes it**: won or led 24 of 30, won outright 14, lost none. Even to ~14:00,
   then the Crown pulls away (halls 6.7 v 2.6 at 20:00).
5. **The Brood mirror has a spot-A edge again** (18 v 7, t 2.3; the brood-spot-study fix had removed it on 0.38.36). With
   the nerf it is smaller (18 v 10, t 0.9). The other mirrors show no edge past chance (the Crown mirror leans to spot B,
   17 v 11, p 0.34, as wr1000 found).
6. **Mirrors end more often**: Brood 21-23 of 30 decided, Clockwork 22, Crown 12 (was 9).

## Caveat: the spots

The shared seed rule (spot = (i div 6) mod 2) put 18 games on spot A and 12 on spot B in every cell, not 15 and 15. In
the matchups between factions that tilts the plain totals a little toward the side on spot A (the first side). The part
reports give the by-spot splits.
