# The campaign goes worldwide: where each continent comes in (Acts 2 and 3)

The owner, 2026-11-22 20:35: "It would be interesting to expand the campaign to be worldwide. Africa, Asia, Oceania, all
the continents." Then 2026-11-22: "Yes" to an outline. THIS IS AN OUTLINE, NOT A MISSION PLAN: where each continent
comes in, which line carries it, the shape of its missions, and what the engine and the art need. The mission plans
(like docs/campaign_act2_plan.md) come later, one act at a time. ALL NAMES AND TITLES ARE WORKING ONES.

What stays fixed (CLAUDE.md, docs/campaign_structure.md, docs/campaign_timeline.md): the King tells it truthfully;
the Crown and the Church are the good side; the Brood is not a person (no creature has a mind, a will or a name of its
own; the Naturalist always tells its story; no Brood hub, no Brood war map); the Clockwork's runaway machine is the
whole faction; three crossings (Rome 2770, Brazil 2780, the global war about 2790); the finale in the present (about
2795); no naval in the engine (seas are crossed off-screen, in stills and on the war map).

## The idea in one paragraph

The Crown is a world empire with its capital in Rome and colonies round the globe. Act 1 is Europe and the Americas;
Act 2 is Brazil, where both dangers are born. The worldwide part is how they LEAVE Brazil: not by marching but by
TRADE. A ship carries a crate of "mining machines" from Concórdia and the Machine's seed wakes in a foreign port; a
ship carries a hold of eggs from the Amazon (smugglers, collectors, the Assembly's men) and the Brood comes ashore in
another jungle. So the dangers follow the Crown's own sea roads: Brazil to Africa (the Atlantic), Africa round the
Cape to the Indies, the Indies to the far colonies of the south. Act 2 shows the first sparks abroad (two short
missions); Act 3, THE FALL OF THE COLONIES, is a tour of the world in fire: each continent a theatre on the war map,
colonies falling one by one, and the player cannot save them all. The third crossing is not in one place: THE NIGHT
THE WORLD CAUGHT FIRE, three missions on three continents on the same night, played in any order.

## The rule of the climate (proposed: it shapes every continent)

- THE BROOD IS REPTILIAN: it thrives in hot, wet lands (jungle, river, mangrove), it spreads slowly in dry heat
  (savanna, desert), and it CANNOT LIVE IN THE COLD (winter stops it; snow kills its eggs). The cold lands are safe
  from it: Canada, Patagonia, the high mountains, Siberia, the far south.
- THE CLOCKWORK NEEDS METAL AND HATES THE WET: it spreads where there are mines and foundries (gold, silver, iron,
  copper), and RUSTS in jungle and monsoon (its wind runs down faster, its plates seize).
- THE CROWN holds the temperate middle, the ports and the seas.

So the global map draws itself: a green belt round the tropics, red stains on the mining lands, purple on the coasts
and in the cold. In missions it is a MISSION RULE only (a mission's `climate`: the Brood's eggs hatch slower in the
cold, the Clockwork's wind runs down faster in the wet); skirmish balance is untouched (Claude's choice 1).

leaders, not only colonists: this is the Church's world, and it makes every theatre a defence of a home, not only of
a colony. Proposed (all invented names, historical places):

## ACT 3 (2781-2790): THE FALL OF THE COLONIES

### The shape

Act 3 is the war map's act. The map grows to the WORLD view with five theatres (AFRICA, THE INDIES, THE SOUTHERN
SEAS, THE AMERICAS, EUROPE) and a CLOCK: each year of the act a few colonies fall unless the player has fought there.
The player picks the theatre; each theatre is a short line of 3 to 4 missions in order; the theatres are free of each
other. A colony that falls is not a defeat screen: it turns green or red on the map, its people come home as refugees
(a ledger line), and it may come back as a RECLAIM PART in a later mission. No player saves every colony: which ones
were held is the ledger the two endings read (ROME HOLDS / ROME FALLS, THE FAITH CARRIES ON).

The three lines go on through the theatres:

- THE CROWN: Tomasz (a bishop now, in his forties) and the generals (Ruggieri after Brazil; Fairleigh, old, one last
  time in Virginia); the Church sends him where the faithful are in danger.
- THE CLOCKMAKER: Hollweg turns against what he made. After Act 2 (Concórdia lost to the Machine) he goes east with
  Ferrer after the seed, and ends Act 3 in the King's palace (where c01 found him telling his story).
- THE NATURALIST: Valadares (the Crown's naturalist, the swarm's hunter) tells the Brood's story in Africa and the
  south; Lindqvist is TAKEN in Act 3 (proposed: in Africa, following the eggs, captured by the Crown: how he comes to
  be a prisoner before the King).

Proposed size: 16 missions in the theatres + the 3 missions of the crossing (19; Act 2 has 11 + 2). The owner has
said "medium to large"; the number can shrink by dropping a mission from each theatre.
