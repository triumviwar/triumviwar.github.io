# The battle matrix: tables

6482 fights. Each cell: **the Crown's share of the value lost** in the fight, as a percent (the Brood's losses over both sides' losses: 50 = an even trade, above 50 the Crown traded better), then the Crown's wins out of the fights (a win = the other side has nothing left that fights, or is clearly behind at the 3-minute bell). Bold: the Crown clearly ahead (the 95% interval above 50); italic: the Brood clearly ahead; plain: not told apart from even.
