## Act 2 in plain words

In the spring of 2771 Hollweg leads the guild out of Fürth by night, with the drawings (or his memory) and every
machine that can walk, before the Crown's commissary can enforce Rome's verdict. He takes ship at Hamburg (a still)
for Brazil, where the two crates he sent "for the mines" in 2767 lie waiting in a mine on the southern highlands. There
he founds a free city of gears, CONCÓRDIA (proposed), with a council of masters (and, if Varenne was spared, Varenne as
its secular voice). The colonists hate it on sight. Hollweg opens the crates: the guild's best work, and inside it the
Perpetual's seed, asleep. By 2777 the city's automata are meshing in the night and voting in the council through the
masters who wind them. Hollweg believes to the end that the city is his; the King knows it never was.

Lindqvist goes home to the Amazon with the specimens Rome ordered destroyed. On a hidden fazenda on an island of the
lower Amazon (proposed: below Óbidos) he breeds them in secret from the cages (a05) and Linnea's own eggs (b01's clutch
saved), believing his lures and pens make them his. In 2772 the Crown's commission (Valadares, carrying Rome's order)
searches the island and does not find everything. In 2775 LINNEA comes down the rivers for her young; in Lindqvist's
pens she grows into what the Brood had been waiting for, a QUEEN, and every bred creature goes to her. The fazenda
falls in a night. The Brood is born. By 2778 it is coming down the rivers, and Valadares holds the Barra.

Between them the Crown holds Brazil. Tomasz, a missionary now, sees the Assembly's ideas reach the gold mines of Minas
(the conspiracy of 2772) and then the swarm reach his own mission village on the frontier (2776). In 2780 the Crown's
army of Brazil, under General Ruggieri (proposed), marches to the crossroads of the São Francisco, where the two fires
meet: a swarm on the Amazon border, a city of gears on the southern one, and one army. THE SECOND CROSSING: the Crown
chooses which to fight first. Both fronts are played; the one fought second has grown.

Act 2 ends with neither danger beaten: the act's name is the BREAKING, the Crown's hold on the world cracking. Act 3
(the Fall of the Colonies) begins on the war map as Act 2 leaves it.
