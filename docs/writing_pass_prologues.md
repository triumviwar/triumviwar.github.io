# Writing pass: the three prologues

Job writing-prologues, 26 November 2026. The owner asked for every campaign line to be checked for the marks of AI
writing, and made as good as it can be. This job did the three prologues: Virginia (p01_leaf), Nuremberg
(c01_thirteenth) and the Rio Negro (b01_specimen), and looked at the tutorial.

Fifty-seven texts changed: 52 spoken lines, the two NEW cards in Virginia, and three lines in the Governor's House.
Every plot fact, choice, flag, objective and trigger is the same as before. Portuguese and Spanish were written for
every changed line.

## What I looked for

These were the habits I found most.

1. The moral at the end. A line tells the story well, then adds a neat lesson. Tomasz in Virginia: "I thought I knew
   what evil was." Hollweg: "I do not know if that was wisdom or cowardice." Lindqvist: "That was the first lie."
2. The clever reversal. "That week, Majesty, the forest watched back." "They were never yours, Meister. They were its."
   "There is no thirteenth hour. There is now."
3. Lists of three. "Free of God, free of their King, free of every bond." "Wind itself, mend itself, and make its own
   successor." "Meshed, wound, and making more."
4. Not this, but that. "Brass, not ghosts in wires." "They are not after your scent. They are going to her."
5. Stock phrases. "It is beautiful. That is what frightens me." "He did not raise his voice. He did not need to." "I
   still smell the smoke when I close my eyes." "It is done. I take no pleasure in it."
6. Everyone sounding alike: short, grave, quotable. The gunner, the governor and the priest all spoke in the same
   rhythm.
7. Creatures given a mind. The King said of the young Brood Mother: "She went home", "She called them, and they came
   for her", "It was her children". Lindqvist said of the Sire: "He did not want us. He wanted her."

## What I did, mission by mission

### Virginia, p01_leaf

The King now talks to his heir like an old man by a fire. Before: "You ask how the world came to burn. Sit. Let an old
man light his pipe first. The leaf is from Virginia. It is fitting." After: "Sit down. You asked me how it began, and
I will tell you, but an old man lights his pipe first. There. Virginia leaf. That is where it began."

The golden age is shown, not stated. Before: "one faith, one law, one King. We were not perfect. But we were at
peace." After: "you could sail from Rome to the last islands of the Pacific and hear the same Mass in every port, and
pay for your supper with the same coin. My father thought it would always be so. So did I."

He tells the heir that Tomasz is the Cardinal they know. His ending keeps the old sense with less speech: "Rome sent
him its thanks and a medal, and we thought that was the end of it. My pipe has gone out."

Tomasz remembers things, not lessons: the smell of the curing leaf, Augustine under the oak, the field hands singing.
At the first death he says the act of contrition into a dying boy's ear, because he is not a priest yet and can give
nothing more. In the fire he carries a little girl out of the smoke, who asks if it is morning yet. Over the dead at
Hollins Farm he says the De profundis. His ending: he went down to the gate with the chaplain, and he has said a Mass
for them every year since.

Fairleigh is dry and hard. His first order now includes Tomasz: take the women and children into the chapel. Tomasz:
"He said it as calmly as grace before dinner. Then he sent for his sword." Of Varenne: "He makes a fine speech, for a
man standing so far back."

Ruggieri sounds Italian: "Madonna santa, a whole year's leaf." Fairleigh's answer: "You shall have one of mine. Now
load."

Varenne is an orator, not a chant. "No king but the people! Burn the leaf of the tyrants!" became "Citizens! You grew
that leaf with your sweat, and a king you will never see smokes it! Burn it!" His other slogans were kept: they are
his trade.

The NEW cards now say plainly what to do: keep Musketeers side by side in front of the guns; round shot flies farther
than the gunners can see, so keep men in front to spot for them.

### Nuremberg, c01_thirteenth

This mission was already the best written of the three; the changes are smaller.

The King: the clockmaker came "in a borrowed coat, with two fingers black from the frost ... You have passed him on
the stairs."

The quarrel is now a quarrel. Ferrer does not recite lore ("You know what destroyed the apostasy ... in the post-modern
age"); he says "You have read what the old age built, Meister. Machines that thought. You know what they did to the men
who built them." Hollweg answers proudly: "This is brass. I cut every tooth of it with my own hands. And if a wheel
could learn a little, in time, would that be so wicked?" Ferrer: "The Church still calls it the ghost in the machine. Do
not build a house for it."

Why nobody knew: "In Nuremberg they still cross themselves when a mill wheel turns with no miller by it. Only Father
Ferrer knew. He was the one man in the city I could not lie to."

Ferrer at the gift: "It is very fine, Meister. Tonight I will say a rosary that I am wrong about it."

At the stroke, Hollweg's line is disbelief instead of an epigram: "Thirteen? There is no thirteen. There is no such hour
on any dial."

The King's last word: "It chose nothing. Mould on bread chooses nothing either."

### The Rio Negro, b01_specimen

The main fix: the King never gives the creatures a mind, and he corrects Lindqvist plainly.

1. Before: "He wrote that his lure called them. It did not. She called them, and they came for her." After: "His lure
   did nothing. The beasts came because the young one cried out every evening, the way a calf bawls and brings the
   herd."
2. Before: "She was never stolen. She went home." After: "No one stole her from him. Beasts went to a beast that called
   them, and took it back into the forest. There is nothing more in it than that, whatever he writes."
3. Before: "It was her children." After: "And the crate he carried out of the fire held no papers. It held her eggs."
4. His opening now says what his part is: "Let him tell it. Where he is wrong, I will say so."

Lindqvist is a condescending lecturer who thinks nature made the Brood. "My dear Valadares. She is a species no one
has ever described, the newest thing nature has made. In ten years her daughters will be the Crown's cavalry, and they
will not eat hay." After the goats: "That is a breed, Valadares, and a breed can be bred."

He still calls the Brood Mother "she": people may. He no longer says what the creatures want. "He did not want us. He
wanted her" became "My men emptied their muskets into him, and he did not so much as turn his head." "He lay down
beside her like a groom" became "He lay down along the pen like a dog at a door, and would not move."

Valadares no longer says "her children": "Whatever those things are, do not carry them down this river to people."

The eyes at the window are seen, not hinted: "eyes at the forest's edge, high off the ground, and a long way apart."
Her call: "a sound like a heron with its throat cut."

## How it was done

tools/campaign/patch_prologues_writing.py holds every change, with its Portuguese and Spanish. It writes the new
English where the old one was (the generators for Nuremberg and the Rio Negro, the JSON itself for Virginia, whose
generator is older than its JSON, and campaign.json for the Governor's House) and swaps the translation entry. Then the
two generators and tools/i18n_build.py run. The build docs' voice tables were updated to the new words.

## Claude's choices, for the owner to check

1. The changed takes were removed from the game, not kept. Old audio saying different words from the box seemed worse
   than silence. To bring a take back for now: git history before this job.
2. Tomasz is named to the heir as "the Cardinal". The plan has old Cardinal Tomasz alive in the present, so the heir
   would know him.
3. Small new details that are not plot: Fairleigh sends the women and children to the chapel; the Commissary counting
   rivets; Hollweg's frostbitten fingers; Pieter's mother writing twice; the act of contrition, the De profundis, a
   yearly Mass, a rosary. Cut any you do not like.
4. In "When I was a boy", the King speaks of the years before 2767. Act 1's Rome mission calls him "the young King" in
   2770, so he was a young man in Virginia's year; I avoided giving him an age.
5. The Brood briefing still says "Answer her, and take her home." It is an instruction to the player's pack, not a
   creature's wish; I left it.
6. Varenne's "Every barn that burns is a chain broken!" and his other chants were kept. Slogans are how an orator talks
   to a crowd.
7. The King's Study hub lines (shared Rome hub) and the hubs after Nuremberg and the Rio Negro were left alone; they had
   no strong marks, and other jobs may touch the Rome hub.
8. tools/campaign/make_m1_mission.py (Virginia's old generator) still has the old words. It was already older than the
   JSON; running it would undo more than this pass.
