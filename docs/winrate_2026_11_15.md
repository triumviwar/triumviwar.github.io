## How it was measured

- **The 2026-11-12 design exactly** (docs/winrate_2026_11_12.md, the 0.33.7 qa-matches design): `tools/qa_match.gd`, Hard
  OPTIMAL v Hard OPTIMAL, the 19 two-player maps x 2 seeds, the sides swapped between the seeds (in the mirror the same
  faction, so P1 is always spot A), to 15:00. The same tags and seeds as 2026-11-12 (`qa_matches.base()`), run by the new
  `tools/study/winrate_1115.py` (the queue: the three asked pairings map by map, then Brood v Clockwork), two games at a
  time on a 2-core machine, about 3.7 minutes of real time per game. Main at f779b36f (0.38.14).
- **Draws**: no winner at 15:00. The LEADER of a draw: income earned + 2 x army value 10% ahead (the old rule).
  "Won or led" = won, or led a draw.
- **Tables**: `tools/study/winrate_1115_report.py DATA docs/winrate_2026_11_12` (new; the 11-12 rows are that study's raw
  data, re-read by the same tool: its W-D-L and won-or-led counts match the 11-12 report). Income per minute = gold (cogs,
  biomass) earned in the minute before; "earned / bench" = the games' mean earned by then over the bench of
  docs/economy_parity.md (each faction alone with the ideal build, original economy: Crown 4,180 / 9,500 / 13,180 at
  5 / 8 / 10 min, Clockwork 4,354 / 9,796 / 13,865, Brood 4,344 / 9,237 / 14,074). Research times from `tech_at`.
- **Films**: `tools/study/winrate_1115_film.sh` (tools/shot_ai_match.gd with `coach:1 every:30`), two per pairing on the
  same map and seed as a study game. A film does not replay its study game (the match is not deterministic), so a film
  shows a kind of game, not that game.
- **Noise**: 38 games a pairing; a won-or-led count moves by about +-3 between two runs. Per-game ratios: income at 8:00
  sd 0.06-0.13, army at 8:00 sd 0.4-0.7. Only changes with |t| > 2.5 are called real below.
