## How it was measured

- **The 2026-11-15 design** (`qa_matches.base()`: the 19 two-player maps x round 0 and 1, the sides swapped between the
  rounds, Hard OPTIMAL v Hard OPTIMAL, `tools/qa_match.gd`), the same tags and seeds as the 11-12 and 11-15 studies, all
  six pairings, to **12:00** (the Crown mirror to 15:00, then 19 round-0 games again to 20:00). Runner
  `tools/study/winrate_1117.py` (round 0 of every pairing map by map, then round 1), one game at a time on a 2-core
  machine (about 4.5 minutes a game), games under a 50-minute timeout.
- **Tables**: `tools/study/winrate_1117_report.py DATA --csv games.csv --long LONG > tables.md` (new). It re-cuts the older
  studies (11-15: crbr, crcw, brcw, brbr; 11-12: crcr, cwcw; both ran to 15:00) at the same minute, and pairs the games by
  tag. "Earned / bench" = mean earned by then over the bench of docs/economy_parity.md (each faction alone with the ideal
  build). Brood "halls" are not counted by qa_match.gd (its bases are rooted creatures), so the Brood's halls-lost is 0 by
  definition.
- **Noise**: 37-38 games a pairing; a won-or-led count moves about +-3 between two runs; only the paired tests and |t| >
  2.5 are called real below.
- **Films**: `tools/study/winrate_1117_film.sh TAG` (tools/film_coach.gd shots:1 under xvfb-run, on a study game's map,
  seed and sides; to 12:00, the Crown mirror to 15:00). A film is a new game: the match is not deterministic, so a film
  shows a kind of game, not its study game.
- Two machine restarts during the run cut two games each; their half-written folders were removed and the games run again.
