# THE PENDULUM HITS FLYERS (pendulum-air, owner 2026-11-15, docs/pendulum_air.md): every swing also cuts the enemy
# flyers in its arc, for three quarters of the blow, no research.

> A tall clock tower on long legs with two arms, each a heavy weight with a crescent blade: they sweep in turn, like scythes, and every blow cuts EVERY enemy in the wide arc in front of it (never your own units), light units hardest. The blades reach up for flyers in the arc too, for three quarters of the blow. Slow and tough. Made at the Heavy Works (once an Escapement works). Costs Tension.

As lâminas também se erguem contra os voadores no arco, com três quartos do golpe. Lento e resistente. Feito na Oficina Pesada (quando um Escape funciona). Custa Tensão.
